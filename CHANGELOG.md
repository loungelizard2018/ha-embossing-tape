# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-07-27

- Initial HACS-installable release.
- Dynamic entity state, entity attribute, or static text source.
- Deterministic per-character baseline, spacing, and rotation imperfections.
- Photorealistic raised lettering with highlight, shadow, specular lighting, and tape texture.
- Configurable tape, edge, lettering, panel, and screw colours.
- Glossy, satin, and matte tape surfaces.
- Optional black instrument panel and four cross-head screws.
- Responsive sizing and overflow protection for desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- Tap and hold actions for more-info, navigation, URL, toggle, and service calls.
